## 📌 Project Overview  
This repository contains the **Community Service Project Report** titled **“Traditional Health Care Methods”**, submitted in partial fulfillment for the award of the **Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering (AI & ML)** at **Chebrolu Engineering College, JNTU Kakinada**.  

The project focuses on:  
- Exploring **traditional health care systems** (Ayurveda, Homeopathy, Naturopathy, Yoga, Unani, Siddha, Allopathy).  
- Studying **medicinal plants and their uses**.  
- Conducting **community surveys and awareness programs**.  
- Analyzing the **role of traditional health care in modern society**.
